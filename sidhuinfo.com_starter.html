The Artist. The Legacy.

Sidhu Moose Wala was the stage name of Shubhdeep Singh Sidhu, a Punjabi singer, rapper and songwriter from the village of Moosa in Mansa district, Punjab. He studied electrical engineering at Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College in Ludhiana before building a music career. His breakthrough song “So High” helped bring his distinctive Punjabi sound to a worldwide audience. He also acted in Punjabi films and released music through his own label, 5911 Records.

Born: 11 June 1993 · Moosa, Punjab
Remembered: 29 May 2022
